## 0003.1 — destruction measurement harness

DROP 0002's most expensive single discovery — that mesh cost per cell spans four
orders of magnitude — only turned up because the harness existed *before* the
optimisation it was going to judge. Destruction gets the same treatment first,
and for the same reason: nobody can say whether a connectivity search is cheap or
ruinous without a world to run it on.

```sh
cargo run --release -p engine_stress --bin structural            # measure and print
cargo run --release -p engine_stress --bin structural -- --check # compare to baseline
cargo run --release -p engine_stress --bin structural -- --write # regenerate it
```

Ten scenarios, each a structure, a set of anchored cells, and the damage it is
about to take:

| scenario | cells | anchors | volumes | regions | removed | expectation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `supported_column` | 416 | 256 | 3 | 1 | 12 | 0 detached |
| `cantilever` | 1,280 | 1,024 | 4 | 1 | 8 | 1 detached |
| `two_support_bridge` | 2,336 | 96 | 5 | 1 | 784 | 0 detached — one pier is enough |
| `cut_column` | 3,216 | 1,024 | 12 | 1 | 64 | 1 detached: the platform |
| `multi_island` | 624 | 16 | 12 | 1 | 16 | 4 detached |
| `volume_boundary_break` | 1,168 | 192 | 4 | 1 | 32 | 1 detached across the seam |
| `region_boundary_break` | 4,240 | 192 | 16 | **2** | 32 | 1 detached across the seam |
| `material_noise_structure` | 784 | 144 | 3 | 1 | 48 | 1 detached |
| `large_supported_structure` | 25,584 | 4,096 | 32 | 1 | 368 | 0 detached |
| `fragment_storm` | 10,240 | 4,096 | 16 | 1 | 1,024 | many detached |

The three that are easy to under-build, and what each is for:

- **`two_support_bridge`** is the *redundancy* case. Removing a support is not
  removing support, and a classifier that treats "lost a connection" as "lost
  its support" passes every other scenario here.
- **`large_supported_structure`** is the expensive **negative** case. Proving a
  25,584-cell structure is *still* supported means the search cannot stop early;
  a detachment never pays that cost. Any claim that connectivity is cheap has to
  answer to this one.
- **`region_boundary_break`** spans two residency regions, which is where
  "unknown is not empty" will eventually be tested for real.

### Anchors live beside the world, not inside the material

Anchors are carried as plain cell positions in the fixture rather than as a
property of `STONE`. That is the scope's rule made structural: **support is world
topology, not a material stat**, and the harness would quietly undermine it by
modelling anchoring any other way. World-side anchor storage arrives in 0003.3;
the scenarios will then hand their anchors to it rather than carrying them.

### What this pass can honestly measure

Most of `StructuralCounters` is **zero**. The engine that fills it in does not
exist yet, and the fields are present from the first commit for the reason
DROP 0002's were: a later pass should show up as a reviewable diff on a file that
was already being compared, not as a new file nobody compares against. A test
asserts they stay zero, so the pass that fills one in cannot do it by accident.

What *is* measured now is the thing everything else depends on — **the scenario
worlds themselves**, plus the static render geometry left after the damage. If a
structure's generation ever drifts, every downstream number drifts silently with
it; pinning the worlds first means a later connectivity change can be told apart
from a later fixture change.

### What it found immediately

`two_support_bridge` was cutting 1,152 cells where its pier only had 784: the
footing is wider than the pier it carries, and one box covering both half-missed.
The test that caught it asserts every damage cell lands on an occupied cell — a
scenario whose damage misses would measure nothing while looking like it measured
something, and would have reported a confident "0 detached" for entirely the
wrong reason.

The baseline is committed to `fixtures/stress/structural.json` and asserted in
`cargo test`. The geometry baseline is untouched: a destruction change and a
meshing change must never land in one diff and have to be told apart by eye.
